## Why sponsor Simple JWT Login

A small monthly budget with a direct effect on a plugin your team or clients already use.

### Reach WordPress developers

Put your brand in front of developers building headless WordPress, mobile apps, and REST API integrations.

### Secure the stack you rely on

If your product or clients depend on JWT authentication in WordPress, sponsoring keeps it maintained and secure.

### Show open-source leadership

Back a free and open-source plugin with no paywall - a visible signal to the developer community.

## What sponsorship funds

Every dollar goes back into the project.

### Security & maintenance

Security reviews, fast patches and compatibility with new WordPress and PHP releases.

### New features

Development time for REST API, WPGraphQL and WP-CLI features requested by the community.

### Docs & SDKs

Documentation, code examples and the official PHP and JavaScript SDKs.

### Infrastructure

Hosting for simplejwtlogin.com, demos, and CI for automated testing.
